# Survivors

A browser RTS. Run with `npm install` then `npm run dev`. Serves on http://127.0.0.1:4317/. 1 vs 1 is an 80×40 field. Free-for-all is 80×70.

Computer sides in 1 vs 1 and free-for-all, and the `ai control` command, all use SiloAI. It gathers Dumps, raises Storage, a Factory, and Pillars, keeps Hovers scouting, and attacks the enemy Silo. When the Dumps beside its Silo run out, or a Hover (or any sight) finds a heap far from its Silos and Storages, it builds a Storage there and hauls to it. A Storage under construction does not protect Stalkers. SiloAI raises a remote one when that ground is inside its Silo, Flasher, or Gorilla sight, or when no enemy has been seen there, and it sends a spare Flasher or Gorilla first. If the builder is captured or an enemy shows up close, the other Stalkers leave. A Pillar captures an uncovered enemy Stalker. SiloAI builds Pillars toward its Dumps and Storage, and pulls Stalkers off ground a known enemy Pillar can see. Stalkers widen a passage that pinches below two cells. Gorillas are built for sieges and sent at buildings; Flashers take units. Combat units muster beside the Silo and attack together in waves. SiloAI leaves two open cells between its own buildings. It only places a building where that side has already explored the footprint and those two cells. Explored ground still under the shroud is allowed. A Hover is sent to chart a black site first. A human Stalker can still build on unexplored ground. Destroying a Silo still captures that side. The wreck stays on the map as a neutral Ruined Silo. A Stalker can repair it for 80 dumps in 8 seconds (the Silo itself has no build price; 80 is the Storage price), and it returns at full health as that Stalker's Silo. When an enemy unit damages the Silo or a building beside it, or steps into weapon range, every Flasher and Gorilla breaks off to attack them, then goes back to the rally. Idle Stalkers mine, build, or repair instead of standing still. Cells are counted and do not limit training or building. Pillar health is 160 and it builds in 7 seconds. Rocks have 28 health and trees have 14.
