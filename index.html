Field notes

How to hold the ridge

  • Left-click a friendly unit or building. Drag a box to select several. Shift adds.
  • Double-click a unit to grab every visible one of that type.
  • Right-click ground to move, an enemy to attack, minerals to gather, or gray rock or a tree with workers to break it. Bikes and tanks cannot break rock or trees, and shots cannot pass through either. Up to 3 workers work each minerals patch; extras take the nearest revealed patch they can reach. When those patches are mined out, workers break the rocks that open the next minerals soonest. Workers break rock or trees that seal their patch, then haul. Each match places the two Castles in opposite corners, with the nearest minerals on the edge of that Castle's sight. Units steer around each other. A worker who is boxed in for 4 seconds goes to the next revealed patch or building site. Dunmarch’s army tries to destroy your Castle once it has seen it.
  • Unexplored ground is black and hides everything. Ground you have seen stays dark gray: rocks, trees, and minerals stay on that ground, and enemies stay hidden until a unit or building is watching that tile. Workers only walk themselves to minerals you have already found. Dunmarch has the same units and the same fog. Its Drones keep scouting the black for minerals and the far map for your Castle, then the gray ground, and they stay clear of Bikes.
  • Enter opens the command line. Off FOW reveals the map, and FOW hides it again. God Mode stops damage to your units and buildings, and Off God Mode lets them take damage again. Show Enemy Cam draws only what Dunmarch can see, and Off Enemy Cam returns to your sight. Both Cam draws both sides at once, and Off Both Cam returns to your sight. AI Control lets the computer play Northmark from your own sight, and Off AI Control gives those orders back. Escape closes the line.
  • Q, then left-click, orders an attack-move. X stops.
  • The match is won by destroying the enemy Castle and lost if your own Castle falls. Dunmarch keeps a few fighters and Towers at home, and pulls the army back when that Castle or a building beside it is under attack.
  • Y places a Factory, L a Silo, and T a Tower. A Tower is one cell and sees as far as a Drone. It automatically shoots the closest enemy in that sight with a Bike's damage and rate, including half damage to Drones, and keeps that target until they leave sight or die. Rocks and trees block the shot. The placement ring is Castle sight: green ground inside it will take a Tower, red ground will not. From the Castle, the nearest idle Worker walks to the site. If every Worker is hauling, the nearest miner goes instead. A building rises only while a Worker is on the site. If that Worker dies or is sent away, the next free Worker takes the job.
  • P trains a Worker from the Castle. K trains a Bike, J a Tank, and H a Drone, when a Factory is selected. A Drone flies over the ground, does not shoot, and sees as far as a Castle. Tanks cannot attack it. Bikes and Towers can, for half damage.
  • Pause freezes the match: units, mining, building, training, and Dunmarch all wait. The Pause button sits beside Controls, and the Pause key does the same. Resume continues from that moment. Orders given while paused are ignored. The command line still opens.
  • WASD or the arrow keys pan. Wheel zooms. Edge of the map scrolls.
  • Middle-drag pans. Click the minimap to jump. Space recenters.
  • F selects idle workers. Ctrl+1–9 saves a group, 1–9 recalls it.
  • Right-click with a building selected to set its rally point. M mutes.

Touch: drag to pan, tap to select, tap the ground to move, tap an enemy to attack.
