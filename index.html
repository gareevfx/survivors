The world has run out of fresh resources, and what's left is the wreckage. Piles of technical debris, called Dumps, lie scattered across the map. Each player starts with a single Silo, the command center that trains Stalkers. These hardy scavengers can't be destroyed. They haul Dumps back to the Silo or to Storages that hold your Cells, and they clear rocks and trees from their path as they go.

Stalkers aren't safe from everyone. Anyone can capture one that wanders outside the watchful range of a Silo, Flasher, Gorilla or Storage. Pillars, the windmills with battle towers, can also capture them. So you expand carefully. Factories turn out Flashers, which are lightning-fast raiders. They also build Gorillas, the heavy tanks that smash buildings, and Hover scouts that map the unknown, because nobody can build in uncharted land.

Everything comes down to the Silo. Destroy an enemy's Silo and you capture it, along with every building, every unit and every resource that player owned. In a 1v1 on a tight 80x40 map, that ends the match. In the four-player free-for-all on 90x50, the war goes on. Even a ruined Silo isn't gone for good, since a brave Stalker can repair it.

Each match is a race between scavenging and fighting. The SiloAI plays by the same rules and never lets a Stalker idle. It builds Storages beside fresh Dumps and keeps a tidy gap between its buildings. It widens narrow passages and gathers its army before it strikes. You can face it as an opponent, or hand it your own side as an autoplayer.
