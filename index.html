Field notes

How to hold New Ground

  • Left-click a friendly unit or building. Drag a box to select several. Shift adds.
  • Double-click a unit to grab every visible one of that type.
  • Right-click ground to move, an enemy to attack, dumps to gather, or gray rock or a tree with stalkers to break it. Flashers and gorillas cannot break rock or trees, and shots cannot pass through either. Up to 3 stalkers work each dumps patch; extras take the nearest revealed patch they can reach. When those patches are mined out, stalkers break the rocks that open the next dumps soonest. Stalkers break rock or trees that seal their patch, then haul. Each match places the two Silos in opposite corners, with the nearest dumps on the edge of that Silo's sight. Units steer around each other. A stalker who is boxed in for 4 seconds goes to the next revealed patch or building site. Silo_02’s army tries to destroy your Silo once it has seen it.
  • Unexplored ground is black and hides everything. Ground you have seen stays dark gray: rocks, trees, and dumps stay on that ground, and enemies stay hidden until a unit or building is watching that tile. Stalkers only walk themselves to dumps you have already found. Silo_02 has the same units and the same fog. Its Hovers keep scouting the black for dumps and the far map for your Silo, then the gray ground, and they stay clear of Flashers.
  • Enter opens the command line. Off FOW reveals the map, and FOW hides it again. God Mode stops damage to your units and buildings, and Off God Mode lets them take damage again. Show Enemy Cam draws only what Silo_02 can see, and Off Enemy Cam returns to your sight. Both Cam draws both sides at once, and Off Both Cam returns to your sight. AI Control lets the computer play Silo_01 from your own sight, and Off AI Control gives those orders back. Escape closes the line.
  • Q, then left-click, orders an attack-move. X stops.
  • The match is won by destroying the enemy Silo and lost if your own Silo falls. Silo_02 keeps a few fighters and Pillars at home, and pulls the army back when that Silo or a building beside it is under attack.
  • Y places a Factory, L a Storage, and T a Pillar. A Pillar is one cell and sees as far as a Hover. It automatically shoots the closest enemy in that sight for 8/3 damage, at a Flasher's rate. Hovers take half of that. It keeps that target until they leave sight or die. Rocks and trees block the shot. The placement ring is Silo sight: green ground inside it will take a Pillar, red ground will not. From the Silo, the nearest idle Stalker walks to the site. If every Stalker is hauling, the nearest miner goes instead. A building rises only while a Stalker is on the site. If that Stalker dies or is sent away, the next free Stalker takes the job.
  • P trains a Stalker from the Silo. K trains a Flasher, J a Gorilla, and H a Hover, when a Factory is selected. While a Silo or Factory queue has something in it, Cancel (last order) drops the newest job, refunds its full dumps cost, and frees its cells. The button is hidden when the queue is empty, and it has no hotkey. A Hover costs 75 dumps and no cells. It flies over the ground, does not shoot, and sees as far as a Silo. Gorillas cannot attack it. Flashers and Pillars can, for half damage.
  • C or the Scanner button, with one or more Hovers selected, sends them through the black fog. Each flies to the nearest unexplored ground, and once the map is explored they revisit the tiles seen longest ago. Several Hovers split up. The button stays lit, and the panel says Scanning. Press Scanner again, give a move order, or press X to stop scanning.
  • Pause freezes the match: units, mining, building, training, and Silo_02 all wait. The Pause button sits beside Controls, and the Pause key does the same. Resume continues from that moment. Orders given while paused are ignored. The command line still opens.
  • WASD or the arrow keys pan. Wheel zooms. Edge of the map scrolls.
  • Middle-drag pans. Click the minimap to jump. Space recenters.
  • F selects idle stalkers. Ctrl+1–9 saves a group, 1–9 recalls it.
  • Right-click with a building selected to set its rally point. M mutes.

Touch: drag to pan, tap to select, tap the ground to move, tap an enemy to attack.
