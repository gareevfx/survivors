Field notes

How to hold the ridge

  • Left-click a friendly unit or building. Drag a box to select several. Shift adds.
  • Double-click a unit to grab every visible one of that type.
  • Right-click ground to move, an enemy to attack, ore to gather, or gray rock to break it.
  • Q, then left-click, orders an attack-move. X stops.
  • Y places a Factory, L a Silo. P trains a Miner from the Castle.
  • K trains a Bike, J a Tank, when a Factory is selected.
  • WASD or the arrow keys pan. Wheel zooms. Edge of the map scrolls.
  • Middle-drag pans. Click the minimap to jump. Space recenters.
  • F selects idle miners. Ctrl+1–9 saves a group, 1–9 recalls it.
  • Right-click with a building selected to set its rally point. M mutes.

Touch: drag to pan, tap to select, tap the ground to move, tap an enemy to attack.
